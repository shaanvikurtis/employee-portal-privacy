About This Policy

Employee Portal is an Android employee self-service application that allows authorized employees to access employment-related information, including attendance, salary details, salary slips, bonuses, advances, loans, documents and notifications.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, used, stored and protected when you use the application.

1. Information We Access or Process

Personal and Employee Information

  • Employee name and employee identification number.
  • Registered mobile number.
  • Company, branch, department and designation information.
  • Employment-related profile information.

Authentication Information

  • Mobile number used for OTP authentication.
  • Firebase Authentication user identifier.
  • Authentication and session-related information.

Employment and Financial Information

  • Attendance records and working days.
  • Salary and salary adjustment records.
  • Bonus, advance and loan information.
  • Salary increase history and employee ratings.
  • Employment-related documents and salary slips.

Device and Technical Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens.
  • Device and application information.
  • IP address and network-related information.
  • Crash, diagnostic and performance information.
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising interaction information, where permitted.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate authorized employees.
  • Display employee profile and company information.
  • Provide attendance and salary information.
  • Generate and display salary slip PDFs.
  • Display bonus, advance and loan records.
  • Deliver relevant employee notifications.
  • Maintain application security and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve application performance.
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Employment and financial records are used to provide employee services and are not used by us to select advertisements.

3. Firebase Services

The application uses Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Mobile number and OTP-based employee authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: Retrieval and display of authorized employee records.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Delivery of employee notifications.

Firebase services process information according to Google's applicable privacy and security terms.

Firebase Privacy and Security Information

4. Google AdMob and Advertising

Employee Portal uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The advertising formats currently integrated include:

  • App Open Ads
  • Banner Ads
  • Interstitial Ads

Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers, device information, app interactions, advertising interactions and diagnostic information for advertising delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Depending on applicable consent settings and regional requirements, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used where applicable to manage advertising privacy choices and consent.

Google AdMob Privacy Information

Google Privacy Policy

5. Information Sharing

Information may be processed or shared with:

  • Authorized Employer Administrators: For legitimate employment and administrative purposes.
  • Google Firebase: Authentication, database and notification services.
  • Google AdMob: Advertising delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • User-selected applications: When an employee voluntarily shares or opens a PDF through another application.

We do not sell employees' personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

6. Salary Slip PDFs and Local Storage

Salary slip PDFs generated by the application may be temporarily stored in the application's local cache or storage.

When an employee chooses to open or share a PDF, the file may be provided to the selected Android application.

Cached files may remain until removed by the application, the user or the Android operating system.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed through the application.

  • Firebase Authentication.
  • Authorized employee access.
  • Company-specific data access restrictions.
  • Secure communication using HTTPS where supported.
  • Security controls provided by Firebase and Google services.

However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Employee records are maintained according to the employer's business, employment and legal record-retention requirements.

Authentication records, notification registration information and technical information may be retained as necessary to provide services, maintain security and comply with legal obligations.

Salary slip PDFs stored in temporary application cache may remain until removed by the application, the user or the operating system.

9. Account and Data Deletion Requests

Employee accounts may be provisioned and managed by the employer or an authorized administrator. The application does not necessarily create independent employment accounts.

If you wish to request deletion of your Employee Portal account and associated personal information, you can submit a request using the contact email below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to the privacy contact listed below.
  2. Use the subject: Employee Portal – Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered mobile number and employee ID so that your request can be verified.
  4. Do not include passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive documents.
  5. Your request will be reviewed and coordinated with the relevant employer or authorized administrator.

After verification, eligible account and personal information will be deleted or anonymized where applicable, subject to employment, financial, legal and other lawful retention obligations.

Certain records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate employment recordkeeping requirements.

Uninstalling the application alone does not necessarily delete server-side employee records.
